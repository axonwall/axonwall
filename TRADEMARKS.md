# Axonwall Trademark Policy & Guidelines

This document outlines the policy regarding the use of the **Axonwall** name, wordmark, logo, and related brand identifiers.

---

## 1. Purpose of This Policy

The **Axonwall** codebase is licensed under the permissive **Apache License, Version 2.0**. Section 6 of the Apache 2.0 License explicitly states:

> *"This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file."*

The purpose of this trademark policy is to ensure that users, developers, and commercial hardware buyers know with certainty whether a network security appliance or software image is an **official, tested, and certified Axonwall release**, or a community fork/third-party derivative.

---

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Prior Approval Required)

You may use the Axonwall wordmark without prior written consent in the following scenarios:

1. **Factual and Truthful Attribution:**
   - Describing your project as *"Powered by Axonwall"* or *"Built with Axonwall components"*.
   - Stating that your software or hardware is *"Compatible with Axonwall rulesets and API"*.
2. **Community Discussion & Education:**
   - Writing articles, tutorials, blog posts, documentation, books, or conference presentations about Axonwall.
3. **Linking to Official Resources:**
   - Using the wordmark to link to the official repository (`github.com/axonwall/axonwall`) or website (`axonwall.com`).

---

## 3. Uses Requiring Prior Written Authorization

To protect community members from security risks and counterfeits, the following uses are **strictly prohibited without prior written consent**:

1. **Commercial Hardware Pre-Installs (Appliance Distribution):**
   - You may not manufacture or sell physical router/firewall hardware branded as an *"Axonwall Appliance"* or *"Official Axonwall System"* unless certified under the Axonwall Hardware OEM Partner Program.
2. **Modified Binary Distribution Under the Same Name:**
   - If you distribute a modified version of Axonwall (e.g. custom kernel, modified web console, or bundled third-party proprietary modules), you **must rename the distribution** and replace official logos so users are not misled into believing they are running an officially tested Axonwall build.
3. **Domain Names & Company Names:**
   - You may not register domain names, trademarks, company names, or social media handles containing "Axonwall" (e.g., `axonwall-cloud.com`, `axonwall-security.org`) in a manner likely to cause confusion.
4. **Endorsement or Certification Claims:**
   - You may not state or imply that your consultancy, hosting service, or managed IT service is *"Certified by Axonwall"* or an *"Axonwall Gold Partner"* without formal certification.

---

## 4. Guidelines for Community Forks & Derivatives

We actively encourage community experimentation, customization, and forking under the Apache 2.0 License:

- When releasing a fork, please choose a distinct name (for example, *"ApexWall - A fork of Axonwall"* or *"NovaRouter - Based on Axonwall technology"*).
- Remove the official Axonwall logo and wordmark from the UI splash screen and installer.
- Retain the original copyright notices and `LICENSE` file as required by Apache 2.0 Section 4.
